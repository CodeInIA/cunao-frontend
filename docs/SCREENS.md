# Screens

Every screen of the storefront and the admin panel, captured locally against the real backend and the live Printify catalogue (desktop at 1440 px wide, mobile at 390 px).

The home page and product page figures (orders, clients, designs) are the shop's real numbers. The storefront reviews are demo content. In the admin screenshots, customer names, emails, phones, addresses and order numbers have been replaced with fake data.
